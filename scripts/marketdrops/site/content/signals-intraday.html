Inside a decline: bigger days both ways, momentum within the day, reversal across days

Several short-horizon regularities are documented in declines. The largest up days cluster beside the largest down days. The direction of the first half hour carries into the last half hour more strongly when volatility is high. Mechanical selling by leveraged funds lands at the close. The split between overnight and daytime losses has no stable pattern. The last part of this page separates what is evidence from what is inference.

Overnight against the session

Over the long run nearly all of the index’s gain has come overnight. For SPY (the S&P 500 fund) from June 1993 to November 2026 the study measures +1,239% from close to next open and +14% from open to close. The New York Fed found the overnight gain was largest after closes with heavy selling, and that returns on average were negative from the open until about noon. NY Fed, 2021 It later reported the overnight effect was close to zero in 2021–25. NY Fed, Jul 2026 Part of the measured overnight gain cannot be captured because of wider spreads at the open. Lachance, 2021

The session carried the fall in 1998, 2000–02, 2007–09 and 2018. The night carried it in 2020 and 2022. In the first 40 sessions after the low the session did most of the rising in 1998, 2009, 2018 and 2025.

Gap-ups sold

Across all sessions since 1993, 46% of opening gaps up closed below the open. In the falling phase of ten declines since 1998 the share ran from 38% (2020) to 68% (December 2018): clearly higher than normal in 1998, 2018 and 2025, lower in 2011, 2015–16, 2020 and 2022. The “Smart Money Index”, which treats a weak first half hour and a strong last hour as bullish, has no published test, and its vendor calls its record mixed. SentimenTrader

Intraday momentum

  • On SPY from 1993 to 2013, the return of the first half hour (from the prior close) predicted the return of the last half hour. The share of variation explained was 1.6% overall, 3.3% on the most volatile third of days, and, with the second-to-last half hour added as a predictor, 6.6% in recessions against 1.0% in expansions. These figures are from the 2015 working-paper version. Gao, Han, Li & Zhou, 2018 Much of the effect came from 2007–09.
  • Across more than 60 futures markets from 1974 to 2020, the move up to half an hour before the close predicted the last half hour, and then partly reversed over the following days. The effect appeared when option dealers were positioned so that they had to sell into falls and buy into rises, and it grew with the size of leveraged funds. Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens, 2021

The largest days cluster

  • Seven of the ten best S&P 500 days fell within two weeks of the ten worst. J.P. Morgan, 2024
  • Of the best days from 2003 to March 2025, 42% came in bear markets and another 36% in the first two months of a new bull market. Hartford Funds / Ned Davis
  • In the study’s table the average daily range roughly doubled in declines: 4.8% in the 2020 fall against 0.7% before it; 2.1% against 0.9% in 2025.

Leveraged funds and option expiry

A fund that promises two or three times the index’s daily move must trade near the close in the direction of the day’s move. The amount is (L² − L) × assets × the index’s return, where L is the leverage: 6 times assets times the return for a 3× fund. A Fed study put the flow at about $1bn per 1% index move in 2013, concentrated in the last hour. Tuzun, 2013 Bank estimates of selling into the close: $15.5bn on 13 September 2022, over $20bn on 3 April 2025, and $56.4bn on 5 November 2026. Bloomberg, 14 Sep 2022 Bloomberg, Apr 2025 Saxo, 11 Nov 2026 The last figure rests on one source.

Options that expire the same day are large in volume, but the exchange finds dealers’ hedging of them is at most about 0.2% of index trading, including in April 2025. Cboe, May 2025 The exchange has an interest in that conclusion; academic findings are mixed.

“Turnaround Tuesday” has thin support: after a Monday fall of 1.5% or more since 2010, Tuesday rose just over 70% of the time, on an unstated number of cases. Schaeffer’s, 2021

Evidence
  • Ranges double; the best and worst days sit together, mostly below the 200-day average.
  • First-half-hour direction carried into the close more on volatile days, and mechanical flows at the close push the same way.
  • Overnight against session, and gaps sold against gaps held, varied from decline to decline.
Inference, not evidence These facts fit trend days in both directions better than fading late-day extremes, and they fit a move that reverses over the following days. They give no support to a fixed short bias, since the largest up days occur in the same weeks. Daily-reset leveraged funds lose value when large up and down days alternate, which is the usual condition in a decline. None of this was tested here as a trading method.
