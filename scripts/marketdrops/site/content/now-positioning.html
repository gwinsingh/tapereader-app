Leverage was at or near records going into the fall

Borrowing against shares reached a record in June 2026 and was still 22% higher than a year earlier in October. The Fed reported the highest hedge-fund leverage since its data began in 2013. How much of this has been unwound in November is not yet known: the data arrive with a lag.

Margin debt is money that customers borrow from brokers against their shares. FINRA, the brokerage regulator, publishes the total monthly FINRA margin statistics.

  • Margin debt peaked in June, the same month as the chip index, and did not make a new high when the S&P 500 did in October.
  • The November figure, which will show how much was repaid or forced out, is due in the second half of December.
  • The study’s own long-run margin series puts the total at 2.3% of GDP at the 2026 peak. It was 1.4% at the September 2018 peak, 2.1% in early 2022 and 2.6% in early 2000.
  • For 2018, FINRA’s series fell from about $648bn in September to about $554bn in December, a 14% drop in three months not verified.

Hedge funds

  • The Fed’s Financial Stability Report says hedge-fund leverage reached the highest level since the data began in 2013, with record or near-record leverage in equity strategies, concentrated in a small number of large funds. The underlying data are for the first quarter of 2026 Fed, 13 Nov 2026.
  • At the October meeting Fed staff named high valuations and hedge-fund leverage as the most salient concerns, and several officials pointed to banks’ lending to hedge funds Fed minutes, 18 Nov 2026.

Leveraged funds, retail and flows: thin evidence, no sign of a washout

The few numbers available show leveraged funds still holding tens of billions, individual investors selling on the worst day, and surveys less fearful on 18 November than in September.

Leveraged funds

  • TQQQ, a fund that aims for three times the Nasdaq-100’s daily move, held $28.0bn on 19 November. It lost 20.5% in October ProShares, 19 Nov 2026.
  • On 20 November the Nasdaq-100 fell 5.61% and the chip index 10.73%. Three times those moves is about −17% and −32% in one day, before costs.
  • SOXL, the three-times chip fund, fell 11% on 13 November alone The Glass Desk, 13 Nov 2026. Its assets and flows were not found.

Individual investors and fund flows

  • 5 November: individuals were net sellers of about $358m by mid-afternoon, the most since March 2020, by Vanda Research’s count The Next Web, 6 Nov 2026.
  • 19 November: individuals traded both sides of the Anthropic listing and sold other stocks, AMD most of all, to pay for it Yahoo Finance, 19 Nov 2026.
  • Week to 11 November: US equity funds took in $6.6bn and money-market funds $37.5bn Investomine, 13 Nov 2026.
  • 16 November, one day: $1.73bn into the chip fund SMH and $3.16bn out of the chip fund SOXX Capp Notes, 19 Nov 2026.

Credit

The extra yield on high-yield company bonds over Treasuries was 3.47% on 18 November, close to its one-year high of 3.48% and up from 3.20% on 13 November. The high-yield bond fund HYG fell only 0.7% from 6 October to 20 November. Stress showed in single names, not in the market as a whole: CoreWeave, Intel and Meta each saw the cost of insuring their debt reach a record between 22 October and 5 November.

What could not be found

  • Prime-broker figures (Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley) for hedge-fund gross and net leverage in October and November.
  • Estimates for trend-following funds, volatility-targeting funds and risk-parity funds.
  • Any named fund failure or forced closure.
  • CNN Fear & Greed, put/call ratios, Investors Intelligence, and the NAAIM manager survey (published with a three-month delay).
  • The share of option volume in same-day contracts.
  • Flows into and out of leveraged funds; company buyback activity and blackout dates.
  • Fund flows after 11 November, and any margin-debt figure after October.
What to take from it
  • Margin debt peaked four months before the index did, at a level well above 2018’s relative to the economy.
  • Desks described overall positioning as light and chip positioning as crowded. The price data fit that: chips −36.7%, the equal-weight index −2.3%.
  • Credit markets had barely reacted by 18 November.
Limits Most of the evidence here is either months old (hedge-fund leverage as of March) or a one-line summary of a bank note. The gaps listed above are the measures that normally show whether forced selling has run its course.
