Series

Investor styles

Thirty names, from fund managers with decades of audited returns to television personalities and convicted fraudsters. Each profile answers the same questions: where the returns came from, how ideas are found and sized, how losses are handled, what the record really is, and what an individual can and cannot take from it.

Names are grouped by style so the background is learned once. Each style has a primer that explains its instruments and vocabulary from zero. The profiles then assume only the primer.

Every profile ends with the same section: what carries over to long-term investing, to swing trading over days and weeks, and to day trading. A ranking of all 30, with stated criteria, comes last, once every record has been checked.

3 of 40 parts published: 2 of 30 profiles and 1 of 10 primers. How each profile is researched.
