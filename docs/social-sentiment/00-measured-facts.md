### Why this is the most important finding of the run so far

1. **It is already paid for.** This sits on the existing key. Rung 0 of the
   budget ladder, not rung 1. Nothing to sign up for.
2. **It is backfillable, for free.** The trader's journal begins well inside the
   100%-insights window, so *every trade already journaled* can be scored
   retroactively. The "history" question was scoped as forward-only-but-price-it
   on the assumption history costs money. For news sentiment specifically, **it
   does not.** This should be reflected in the recommendation.
3. **It is cheap to run.** One market-wide call per day returns the whole day's
   news with insights attached. Steady state is ~1 call/day; the 5/min limit is
   irrelevant at that cadence.
4. **It is auditable.** `sentiment_reasoning` means this is not a black-box
   score — the justification can be read, spot-checked, and disagreed with. Per
   the brief's Track A7 criterion, that makes it strictly more valuable than an
   opaque vendor sentiment number.

### Caveats to carry into the analysis

- This is **news** sentiment, not **social/retail** sentiment. It measures what
  journalists and wire services wrote, not what the crowd said. For a breakout
  trader those are related but distinct — news is more likely the *catalyst*,
  social is more likely the *fuel*. Do not let the fact that this one is easy
  cause the study to quietly redefine the question.
- Sentiment is assigned **per article per ticker**, so aggregation to a
  daily per-ticker score requires a stated rule (mean? volume-weighted?
  publisher-weighted? decayed?). That is a Track D decision, not a given.
- Coverage is skewed toward names journalists cover. Thin/small-cap breakout
  candidates may have little or no news — measure the coverage rate across the
  liquid universe before assuming it generalises.
- `sentiment_reasoning` is model-generated by Polygon. Its provenance and
  stability across time are unverified; a change in their scoring model would
  create a silent discontinuity in any long time series. Worth a periodic
  spot-check.
